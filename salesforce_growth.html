What to trust in this reel

  • Personal project built from public information. Views are my own. This is not an official Salesforce publication and not investment advice.
  • Revenue, GAAP operating margin, GAAP EPS and operating cash flow match Salesforce's 10-K financial statements (SEC XBRL pages). Non-GAAP operating income and EPS come from the Exhibit 99.1 earnings releases. Releases before FY22 do not state a non-GAAP margin, so FY15 to FY20 margins are computed from the company's non-GAAP operating income tables. FY15, FY17, FY19, FY21, FY23 and FY25 non-GAAP figures come from the next year's comparative column, so a later restatement cannot be ruled out.
  • The non-GAAP margin definition changed over time: restructuring is excluded from FY23 and acquisition costs from FY25, so the series is not perfectly like for like. FY18 revenue is as reported ($10.48B); Salesforce later recast it to $10.54B under ASC 606. FY21 GAAP EPS of $4.38 includes a one-time tax benefit of about $2.0B.
  • Everything after January 2026 is guidance, not results, from the Aug 26, 2026 release: revenue $46.1B to $46.4B (midpoint shown), non-GAAP margin 34.3%, GAAP margin 20.1%, non-GAAP EPS $16.67 to $16.71 and GAAP EPS $10.21 to $10.25. The non-GAAP EPS guide includes gains on strategic investments and the effect of the $25B buyback on the share count. Operating cash flow is guided to grow 4% to 5%; I applied 4.5%, a figure I derived. The guide assumes the Contentful and Fin deals close. Between year-ends the lines glide in a straight line.
  • Free cash flow is shown from FY25 only; earlier years were not collected. Agentforce ARR comes from the earnings releases of Dec 3, 2025, Feb 25, May 27 and Aug 26, 2026 (the last reads "above $1.5B").
  • Launch and record dates were checked against Salesforce newsroom pages and earnings releases. For several launches only the date shown in a search result was confirmed, and Agentforce's general availability press release is dated Oct 29, 2024 while its availability date was Oct 25. World events are dated from the WHO, central banks and OpenAI announcements; I did not open all of those pages myself.
  • Deal prices come from deal lists and press coverage, not Salesforce filings. Demandware's $2.8B price and Jul 11, 2016 close date come from Salesforce's press releases. Own's Nov 18, 2024 close date comes from Salesforce's press release and its about $1.9B cash price from TechCrunch's report. Braze is pending. Buyback authorizations and the first dividend come from the 10-Ks. Buybacks for FY23 and FY24 are recalled; later years come from cash flow statements.
