What to trust in this reel

  • What moves shares outstanding: it goes up when employees receive shares (RSUs vesting, options exercised, the employee stock purchase plan) and when an acquisition is paid partly in Salesforce stock (MuleSoft, Tableau, Slack), plus one small convertible-note settlement. It goes down when Salesforce buys its own shares back. Salesforce reports the cash it pays for taxes when RSUs vest ($351M in FY26) but not how many shares that covers.
  • Year-end share counts, shares issued to employees and for deals, shares repurchased, repurchase dollars and stock pay come from the equity statements and cash flow statements in Salesforce's 10-Ks for FY16 to FY26 (SEC XBRL pages). Shares outstanding from FY23 on is shares issued minus treasury shares, each rounded to millions, so it can be off by 1M.
  • Quarter-end counts for FY25 to Q2 FY27 are from the 10-Qs and 10-Ks. Between reported dates the line is straight, with jumps on the deal and ASR dates. The late-2022 high of about 1,000M is derived, not reported. The count after Jul 31, 2026 is held flat at the 823M cover-page figure; Q3 results are due Dec 2.
  • The filing lines that show shares issued for acquisitions do not name the deals. I matched them to MuleSoft (FY19), Tableau (FY20) and Slack (FY22) from the dates and dollar values. The FY17 acquisition shares are unnamed.
  • FY27 to date covers the six months to Jul 31, 2026: 114M shares retired (103M from the ASR's first delivery, 11M in the open market at about $192) for $27.4B. The ASR's final settlement was expected around Oct 2026 and I found no filing confirming it, so shares still to be delivered are not counted. New shares issued in FY27 (8M) are derived from the share count.
  • The $240 FY27 average (marked *) is $27.4B divided by the 114M shares delivered so far. If the ASR delivers the remaining ~20% of its shares, the true average will be lower. “Worth now” in the scorecard values the retired shares at the price on the timeline date.
  • \u201cFrom the high so far\u201d compares each number with the highest value seen up to the date shown. The market cap record, $352B on Dec 4, 2024, is a single-day figure; the price that day (about $368) is that cap divided by my modelled share count, so it is an estimate.
  • The share price before Aug 2026 is estimated as market cap divided by an estimated share count, so it can be off by a few percent. The last four month-ends are actual closes. Salesforce does not disclose how many shares are withheld for taxes on vesting RSUs, only the cash ($351M in FY26).
  • Market cap is FinHacker's month-end series (source). The first-dividend and authorization dates come from the 10-K notes; the Sep 2025 date is from an XBRL label and is low confidence.
